01 / THE PROJECT

From Callosa d'en Sarrià to contemporary jazz.

This project starts from a very specific source: the rhythmic and melodic structures of the traditional dances of Callosa d'en Sarrià.

From them, Daniel Buades creates new compositions, spaces for improvisation and soundscapes that connect a community's memory with the language of contemporary jazz.
